11.  List the Departments with More Than Three Employees. 
SELECT D.DNAME,COUNT(E.EMPNO) AS employee_count FROM DEPT D JOIN EMP E ON D.DNO = E.DEPTNO

36. Display the name of the departments that have at least one project with budget above 400000 (correlated).
SELECT D.DNAME
FROM DEPT D
WHERE EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM PROJ P
WHERE P.DNO = D.DNO AND P.PRJ_CREDIT > 400000);


37. Display the names of employee who earn more than the average salary of their own  department (correlated). 
SELECT E.EMPNO, E.ENAME, E.SAL, E.DEPTNO
FROM EMP E
WHERE E.SAL > (SELECT AVG(E2.SAL)FROM EMP E2
WHERE E2.DEPTNO = E.DEPTNO);


38. Display the name of Employees who have no dependents (NOT EXISTS)
